    About the journal

    The Journal for Law Advocacy is the official publication of the West Visayas State University College of Law. It publishes original legal scholarship from students, faculty, and practitioners, with a focus on questions that matter to Philippine courts, communities, and the practice of law.

    Articles are selected and edited by the Editorial Board and reviewed by faculty advisers. The journal is committed to rigorous research, clear writing, and advocacy grounded in the law.

    Submit to the journal

    We accept articles, case comments, and student notes year-round.

    Who can submit

    Students and alumni of the College of Law, faculty members, and practitioners. Edit this to match your journal's policy.

    Manuscript format

    Word document, 5,000 to 12,000 words, with an abstract of up to 200 words. Cite in the format your board follows and include all footnotes.

    Review process

    The Editorial Board screens each manuscript, then assigns it for faculty review. Authors are notified of the decision by email.

    Rights and permissions

    Authors keep their copyright and grant the journal the right to publish the work in print and online.

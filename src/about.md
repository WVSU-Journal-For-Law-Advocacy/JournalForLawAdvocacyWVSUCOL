The **WVSU Journal for Law Advocacy** is the official law journal of the West Visayas State University College of Law in Iloilo City. Founded in 2021 and run by the College's law students with the guidance of its faculty, it publishes legal scholarship by students, professors, and practitioners on the law and its working in the Philippines, written, wherever possible, in plain English.

The Journal takes its name from the College's commitment to advocacy. It was conceived alongside the **WVSU Center for Law Advocacy**, the College's legal aid clinic, where law student practitioners serve indigent clients under the Supreme Court's Revised Student Practice Rule. The Journal carries that work onto the page: careful analysis of the problems people bring to the law, and of how the law could answer them better.

## What we publish

Each volume gathers articles, essays, commentaries, case notes, and comments on recent legislation, procedural rules, and jurisprudence, usually around a theme that matters in its year: elections and democracy, the pandemic years, human rights, the environment, the Bill of Rights in a digital age, and, in the volume now in preparation, public accountability and the fight against corruption.

Submissions are open to law students of every school offering a Juris Doctor program, and to law professors, practitioners, and other legal professionals. See the [submission guidelines](/submit/) and the [call for papers](/call-for-papers/).

## How articles are reviewed

The Journal adopted **double-blind peer review** from its first issue. Manuscripts are anonymized and read by reviewers who are themselves legal professionals, and authors revise their work in light of the reviewers' comments. Student essays are reviewed and edited by the Editorial Board. The standard was set deliberately to match the publishing practice of the sciences and humanities, and it remains the Journal's editorial policy. Details are in the [peer review policy](/policies/peer-review/).

## How the Journal is run

The founders chose not to have a single editor-in-chief. The Journal is led instead by a small **Board of Editors**, headed by an Executive Editor, working together with the **Contribution Editors**, who guide authors through their submissions, and the **Editorial Apprentices**, who are learning the craft. A faculty adviser counsels each board, and each new board is selected through a writing examination and takes an oath of office. Its work is governed by the Journal's Manual of Operations.

## Free to read, ready to cite

Every piece is free to read on this website, with its own page, PDF, and citation in the Journal's house style, Bluebook, APA, or Chicago. Each article also has a public **record of publication** that confirms its authors, volume, and pages, so authors can cite their work with confidence.

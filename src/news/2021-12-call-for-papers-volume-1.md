The West Visayas State University WVSU Journal for Law Advocacy began accepting student-authored papers for its maiden issue, Volume 1, 2022.

The call was open to students of all schools offering a Juris Doctor program. It invited legal articles and commentaries, law book reviews, case notes, and comments on recent legislation, procedural rules, and jurisprudence, preferably in plain English. Every submission went through double-blind peer review.

The original deadline of January 3, 2022 was later extended to February 26, 2022.

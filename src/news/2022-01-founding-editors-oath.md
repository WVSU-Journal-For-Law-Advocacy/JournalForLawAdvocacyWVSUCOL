The first editorial board of the WVSU Journal for Law Advocacy, known as **Batch Banaag**, took its oath of office in what the journal called "a historic spectacle amidst this Age of Omicron."

The founding editors were Clyde Ben Gacayan (Executive Editor), Vanessa Silla (Manuscript Editor), Rene Aviles (Executive Contribution Editor), Edson Chase Yap, Kirby Aguaras, Theda Gumban, and Mike Guillem. Together they wrote the opening chapter of Volume 1.

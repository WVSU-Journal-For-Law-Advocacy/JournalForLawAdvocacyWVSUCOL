The Journal's first printed book brought together its first two volumes. **Volume 1 (2021–2022)** collects six articles on electoral law and the 2022 national elections. **Volume 2 (2022–2023)** adds eight student essays, from vaccination at work and children in conflict with the law to cyber libel, the Anti-Terrorism Act, and the case for a jury system.

The book opens with messages from Dean Pauline Grace B. Alfuente and Associate Dean Ian Thomas M. Besana, a backstory of the Journal's founding by its adviser, Judge Enrique Z. Trespeces, and an Editor's Note by founding Executive Editor Clyde Ben A. Gacayan. It closes by introducing the founding editors and with the call for papers for Volume 3.

The journal congratulated the newly selected Board of Editors and Contribution Editors for its second year, led by Executive Editor Clint Deo N. Genciana.

The board held a checkpoint meeting on June 15, 2023 with Dean Pauline Grace B. Alfuente and Judge Enrique Z. Trespeces, and went on to publish the Second Issue, on human rights, that December.

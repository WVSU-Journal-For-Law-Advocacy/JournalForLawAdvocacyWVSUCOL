*Reported by Mark Lawrence Articuna.*

Juris Doctor students of the WVSU College of Law learned academic writing and the management of a law journal from WVSU alumni at the Moot Court in Quezon Hall on July 22, 2023.

Dr. Hazel P. Villa, an editor of the WVSU Research Journal and adviser of SILAK Media, led a workshop on style for journal writing. She introduced the board to the practices that govern journal submissions and to the symbols used in copyreading. "They are working on the second issue now, but I would like for the Law School journal to reach a level [this early] where we demand only the best from the paper submissions," she said.

Atty. Maria Reylan Garcia, former editor-in-chief of the University of Saint La Salle–Bacolod Law Journal, then spoke on "Managing a Law Journal and Insights on Human Rights in the Philippines."

"This is a great opportunity for us to improve significantly," said Executive Editor Clint Deo N. Genciana. Dean Pauline Grace B. Alfuente and Judge Enrique Z. Trespeces, co-adviser of the journal, also attended. Both have supported the board since its inception in 2021.

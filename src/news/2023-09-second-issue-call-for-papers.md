The Journal for Law Advocacy invited Juris Doctor students of the colleges of law in Iloilo City to contribute to its Second Issue, on **human rights**. The schools were WVSU, Central Philippine University, the University of San Agustin, and UP Diliman's Iloilo City campus. Papers were to examine how laws, jurisprudence, and human rights reforms interact in the Philippines.

As incentives, the journal offered scholarly prizes for the three best articles and certificates for all accepted papers.

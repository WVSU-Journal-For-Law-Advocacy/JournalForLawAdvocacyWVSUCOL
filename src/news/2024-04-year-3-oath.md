The newly selected Board of Editors and Contribution Editors of the Journal for Law Advocacy, Year 3, took their oath on April 4, 2024 at the WVSU College of Law Moot Court. Judge Enrique Trespeces administered the oath of duty.

Dean Atty. Pauline Grace Buñol-Alfuente, Associate Dean Atty. Ian Thomas Besana, Atty. Regina Gustilo, and Atty. Federic Loise Regencia attended. The new members pledged to hold to the standards of academic publishing and legal writing with integrity and excellence. This board would publish Volume 4.

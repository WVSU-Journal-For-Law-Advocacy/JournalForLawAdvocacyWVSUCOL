The editors of the WVSU Journal for Law Advocacy held their 2024 Editorial Workshop under the theme **"Journal Essentials: A Workshop on Scholarly Writing."** The guest speakers were Dr. Hazel P. Villa, Director of Public Affairs, Marketing and Communication at the WVSU Communications Office, and Atty. Regina Johanna V. Gustilo, a full-time faculty member of the College of Law.

In a separate workshop, the members met with their supervising faculty, Judge Enrique Z. Trespeces, and Ms. Vanessa Silla, a former editor of the journal from Batch Banaag.

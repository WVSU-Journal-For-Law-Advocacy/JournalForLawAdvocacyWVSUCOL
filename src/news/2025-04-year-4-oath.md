The Year 4 editorial team held its first meeting and orientation on April 3, 2025, led by Judge Enrique Z. Trespeces. The team set the activity calendar for the year's volume with Dean Pauline Alfuente, Associate Dean Ian Thomas Besana, Atty. Regina Johanna V. Gustilo, Atty. Federic Loise S. Regencia, and two former editors: Atty. Vanessa Silla (Emeritus Manuscript Editor) and Luigi Bien Villanueva (Emeritus Executive Editor).

The new members took their oath on April 24, 2025 at the WVSU College of Law Moot Court. Judge Trespeces, adviser to the journal, administered it. Dean Atty. Pauline Grace Buñol-Alfuente, Associate Dean Atty. Ian Thomas Besana, Atty. Mehelinda Penetrante, Atty. Tina Marie Coo, Atty. Regina Gustilo, and Atty. Federic Loise Regencia attended.

The board was led by Executive Editor Elyn Joy Estimada and published Volume 5.

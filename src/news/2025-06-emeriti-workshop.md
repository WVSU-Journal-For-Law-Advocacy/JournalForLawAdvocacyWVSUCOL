The WVSU College of Law organized an editorial workshop for the executive board of the Journal for Law Advocacy, joined by supervising faculty member Judge Enrique Z. Trespeces.

The resource speakers were three of the journal's founding editors from Batch Banaag: Atty. Vanessa Silla (Emeritus Manuscript Editor), Atty. Clyde Ben Gacayan (Emeritus Executive Editor), and Atty. Rene Aviles (Emeritus Executive Contribution Editor).

On October 9, 2025, the Board of Editors, with the newly recruited Editorial Apprentices, met to plan the publication of Volume 5, "Championing Constitutional Rights: Navigating the Bill of Rights in the Digital Revolution." The meeting covered the articles received, the layout design, and the final arrangements for printing and distribution.

The WVSU Journal for Law Advocacy congratulated its alumni who passed the 2025 Bar Examinations:

- **Atty. Clint Deo Genciana**, Executive Editor
- **Atty. Rodante Navallasca**, Member, Board of Editors
- **Atty. Aimee Andrea Gaje**, Member, Board of Editors
- **Atty. Ryan Cyril Gancia**, Executive Contribution Editor

All four served on the board that published the Second Issue. "As you ascend to the ranks of officers of the court, may your writing skills continue to enhance the legal profession."

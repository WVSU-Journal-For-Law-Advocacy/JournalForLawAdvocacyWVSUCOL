For Volume 6, the journal opened its call to Juris Doctor students of West Visayas State University and of every school in the Visayas Region and Southern Philippines offering a JD program, as well as to the faculty of the WVSU College of Law. The theme was **"Combatting Impunity: A Quest for Public Accountability in the Fight Against Corruption."**

Submissions were limited to 5,000 words and had to follow the Bluebook. The deadline of August 1, 2026 was extended to September 11, 2026. The call is now closed.

The journal's new editorial members and staff for Academic Year 2026–2027 took their oath on May 26, 2026 at the 3rd Floor of the WVSU Research Building. JLA advisers Judge Enrique Z. Trespeces and Atty. Regina Johanna V. Gustilo officiated.

On July 30, the journal held the **5th JLA Editorial Enhancement Lectures and Workshop** at the same venue, with three resource speakers:

- Atty. Edson Chase Yap: "How to Select an Appropriate Topic Within the Theme"
- Atty. Clint Deo Genciana: "Establishing the Structure Standards for Law Journals"
- Atty. Aimee Andrea Gaje: "Honing Your Methodology and Research Skills"

The same day, outgoing Executive Editor Jose Gian Karlo Farrol led the turnover from JLA Year 4 to JLA Year 5. Dean Atty. Ian Thomas Besana, REB, LLM, and the JLA advisers were present.

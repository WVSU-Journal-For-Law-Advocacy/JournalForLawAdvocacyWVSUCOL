title: Volume 2 is now online
date: 2026-10-05
summary: The eight student essays of Volume 2 (2022–2023) can now be read and downloaded, with page numbers added to Volume 1 and the inaugural book preserved in full.
---

Volume 2 of the WVSU Journal for Law Advocacy (2022–2023) is now on this website. Its eight student essays were first published alongside Volume 1 in the Journal's inaugural book:

- [Discretionary Inoculation](/articles/discretionary-inoculation/), by Jezner Ralph D. Sasi, on DOLE's prohibition of "no vaccine, no work" policies.
- [Not Just a Children's Game](/articles/not-just-a-childrens-game/), by Clyde Ben A. Gacayan, on children in conflict with the law during the pandemic.
- [Stories Behind the Numbers](/articles/stories-behind-the-numbers/), by Philipp Centenni L. Ruel, on human trafficking.
- [A Law Written in Blood](/articles/a-law-written-in-blood/), by Vanessa G. Silla, on the Anti-Terrorism Act of 2020.
- [The Philippines' Commitment to the Fight Against Impunity for Atrocity Crimes](/articles/impunity-for-atrocity-crimes/), by Edson Chase H. Yap.
- [Libel in the Cyber Space](/articles/libel-in-the-cyber-space/), by Clint Deo N. Genciana.
- [Framing the Constructs of Public Accountability in Government Service](/articles/constructs-of-public-accountability/), by Ronie C. Cajayon.
- [Are Philippine Courts Ready for a Jury System?](/articles/are-courts-ready-for-a-jury-system/), by Rene M. Aviles, Jr.

Each essay has its own page, PDF, and citation. The pieces of Volume 1 now carry their page numbers in the original book, which is preserved in full on both volumes' pages. The founding editorial board, with the faculty Executive Committee that selected and advised it, is listed as printed in the book.

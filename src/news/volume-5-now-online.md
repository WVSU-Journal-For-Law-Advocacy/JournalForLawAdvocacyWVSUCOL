Volume 5 of the WVSU Journal for Law Advocacy (2025–2026) is now available on this website. Its 23 pieces examine rights and liberties in a digital age: privacy and data protection, freedom of expression, artificial intelligence in the courts and the legal profession, elections, and the right to information.

Each article has its own page with an abstract, a PDF of the article, and a citation you can copy or download for Zotero, Mendeley, or EndNote. The complete volume is also preserved as originally published.

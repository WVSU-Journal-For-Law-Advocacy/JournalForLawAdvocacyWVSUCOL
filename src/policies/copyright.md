**Authors keep the copyright** to their work.

By publishing with the journal, authors grant the WVSU Journal for Law Advocacy a non-exclusive license to publish the work in print and online, to include it in the journal's archive, and to make it available under the [open-access license](/policies/open-access/).

## What authors may do

- Post the published version on a personal website, institutional repository, or academic networking site, with a citation and link to the journal.
- Republish the work elsewhere, such as in a book or another journal, as long as the original publication in this journal is acknowledged.
- Use the work in teaching and presentations.

## Third-party material

Authors are responsible for getting permission to reproduce material owned by others, such as images, figures, or long quotations, unless its use is allowed by law.

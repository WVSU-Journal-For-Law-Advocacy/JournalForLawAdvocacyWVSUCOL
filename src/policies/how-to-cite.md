The journal's abbreviation is **J. Law Advoc.** and its ISSN is **1908-532X** (Print). Every article page has a **Cite** box that gives the citation in several formats and lets you download it for Zotero, Mendeley, or EndNote.

## House style

> Author, *Title*, Volume J. Law Advoc. First page (Year).

Example:

> Elmo V. Ador Jr., *CCTV and Privacy in the Philippines: A Need for a Comprehensive Law on CCTV*, 5 J. Law Advoc. 90 (2025).

## Pinpoint citations

To cite a specific page, add it after the first page:

> Elmo V. Ador Jr., *CCTV and Privacy in the Philippines: A Need for a Comprehensive Law on CCTV*, 5 J. Law Advoc. 90, 95 (2025).

## Volume 1

Volume 1 (2021–2022) was published as separate pieces without continuous page numbers. Cite those pieces without a page number:

> Clyde Ben A. Gacayan, *No Time Runs Against Families? Gains and Losses in Regulating Political Dynasties in the Philippines*, 1 J. Law Advoc. (2022).

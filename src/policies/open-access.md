The WVSU Journal for Law Advocacy is an **open-access** journal. Every article is free to read and download, with no subscription, registration, or fee.

## License

Unless an article states otherwise, articles are published under the **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License** ([CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)).

This means anyone may:

- **share** the article: copy and redistribute it in any medium or format; and
- **adapt** it: quote, excerpt, translate, and build upon it,

as long as they:

- **give credit** to the author and the journal, with a link to the article, and indicate if changes were made; and
- do **not use it for commercial purposes**.

## No fees

The journal does not charge authors any fee to submit or publish.

## Archiving

Articles are kept permanently on this website, both as individual PDFs and in the original full-volume PDFs.

The journal is committed to honest and responsible scholarship, guided by the principles of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).

## Authors

- Submit only **original work** that has not been published and is not under review elsewhere.
- **Cite every source** you rely on, including your own prior work. Quotations must be marked and attributed.
- List as authors only those who contributed substantially to the work.
- **Disclose the use of generative AI tools** in a footnote, describing what the tool was used for. AI tools cannot be listed as authors, and authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy of every statement and citation.
- Disclose any financial or personal interest that could be seen to affect the work.

## Plagiarism

Every manuscript is checked for similarity before review. Plagiarism, including copying text without attribution, presenting another's ideas as one's own, or reusing one's own published work without disclosure, results in rejection. If plagiarism is found after publication, the article will be corrected or retracted, and the author's institution may be informed.

## Reviewers

Reviewers keep manuscripts confidential, do not use unpublished material for their own work, give objective and constructive comments, and declare any conflict of interest.

## Editors

Editors judge manuscripts on their merit alone, without regard to the author's identity, affiliation, or beliefs, and keep the review process confidential.

## Corrections and retractions

Errors found after publication are corrected with a dated note on the article page. Serious problems, such as plagiarism or fabricated sources, lead to retraction. A retracted article stays online, clearly marked as retracted, with the reason.
